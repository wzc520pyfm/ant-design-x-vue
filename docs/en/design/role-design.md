# Introduction

![](https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*rOt6Rq4L6qgAAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/fmt.webp)

Role design allows AI to play a certain identity role to match the user's intentions, thereby ensuring that the interaction with the user is smooth and in line with expectations. Character design allows the invisible experience in AI products to be optimized and defined.

In this section, we will explore how to give AI products a clear “role” identity to better enhance user experience.

## WHY role design

In the AI ​​era, characters have become a bridge connecting the physical and digital worlds, profoundly changing human lifestyles and social structures. In this era, characters are no longer limited to fictional images in traditional media such as literature, film and television, but have evolved into agents that integrate advanced artificial intelligence technology and are active in various human-computer interaction scenarios. These intelligent characters not only have traditional character attributes such as well-designed background stories, personality traits, and language styles, but also have advanced functions such as learning capabilities, environmental perception, and emotion recognition and response.

![](https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*KlODQbZ-o3oAAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/fmt.webp)

With the continuous advancement of technology, these characters can continue to optimize themselves through machine learning, achieve deeper emotional communication and intelligent symbiosis, promote a more natural and harmonious interaction mode between humans and intelligent systems, and open a new chapter in human-computer interaction.

![](https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*VlG-RaRy5rIAAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/fmt.webp)

## WHAT is role design

Character components are the necessary foundation for creating a three-dimensional, real, and convincing character. The core needs to pay attention to the character's character core and external image, and when necessary, the character's professional capabilities. Here are the main role building blocks:

![](https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*sB8bRIfxIrQAAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/fmt.webp)

### Appearance

The external image includes appearance characteristics, clothing style, body language and voice characteristics, etc. It is the visual and auditory reflection of its personality and background story. It is designed to intuitively convey the character's characteristics and enhance the first impression and emotional resonance of the audience or user.

![](https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*LHjERb77lp0AAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/fmt.webp)

### Expertise

Professional abilities refer to the knowledge, skills, practical experience and problem-solving expertise mastered in a specific field. These abilities support their professional performance, realize role functions, solve challenges in scenarios, and demonstrate their value and irreplaceability.

![](https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*OtQ6SoMWOCIAAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/original)

### Personality

The core of character is the fundamental driving force for behavior, decision-making and emotional reactions. Including the character's core personality traits, values, belief motivations and emotional state, etc. These traits form the character's behavior patterns and reactions when faced with different situations.

![](https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*YXmlRIMUrQ4AAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/original)

## HOW: principles for role design

The essence of character creation lies in consistency, naturalness and emotion.

**Character consistency:** When creating a character, you need to dig deep into the character's background, motivations and personality traits, give it uniqueness and layering, and ensure that its behavioral logic is consistent with the character setting from beginning to end. <br/> **Character naturalness:** When creating characters, it is necessary to interact closely with the storyline and other characters to promote plot development, make the characters vivid and three-dimensional, and incorporate appropriate historical and social environmental factors to make the characters speak and behave naturally, and enhance the convincingness and resonance of the story. <br/> **Emotional character:** Characters need to have real human emotions and motivations to make them believable and warm. The characters' emotions should change, and their emotions should rise and fall as the story progresses, which makes the characters more real.
