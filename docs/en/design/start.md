# Start

## WHAT overview

When users first interact with AI, the AI should start with a warm greeting and quickly demonstrate its capabilities to leave a positive first impression. The goal is to quickly make users feel confident, feel they can control the conversation, and help them understand what the AI can do for them, rather than giving the impression that they are taking a tutorial. The top priority is to quickly set the right expectations, guide users to discover AI's capabilities, and give control of the conversation back to them.

## HOW to operate

During initial communication, there are three core goals that need to be achieved through greetings:

<ImagePreview>
<img class="preview-img no-padding" src="https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*MOx0TraEUzoAAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/fmt.webp">
</ImagePreview>

1. **Warmly Welcoming Users:** Express a friendly greeting to establish a positive initial connection. For example, greet users in warm and simple ways like "Hello" or "Welcome."

2. **Set Clear Expectations:** Clearly communicate AI's features and capabilities, giving users an accurate expectation of AI's use. You can simply list 2-3 high-frequency features, but avoid going into details.

3. **Empowering Users with Control:** Ensure users feel they are in charge of the conversation. By offering options or directly asking about user needs, let users feel in control of the communication process.
