# Provide intention expectations

![](https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*X2-8QrjSVdEAAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/fmt.webp)

During the interaction between users and AI, a common challenge is that users lack understanding of the capabilities of AI. This often leads to users feeling confused when asking questions. They are not sure how to start a conversation, nor are they sure of the reasonable scope of questions. In view of this current situation, it is particularly important to effectively guide users' intentions, aiming to help users clarify the boundaries of AI's capabilities and thereby establish intention expectations that are consistent with the achievable scope of AI. **

## Best cases

When users enter the world of AI products for the first time, they can understand the powerful capabilities and specific uses of AI by providing ways in which AI can achieve expected intentions.
