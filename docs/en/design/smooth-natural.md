## How to define

Characters demonstrate the fluency and authenticity of human communication when interacting with users. This involves the natural flow of language expression, the use of vocabulary and grammar close to human daily conversation, and the avoidance of mechanical and rigid expressions. At the same time, the character's behavioral responses should also be natural and reasonable, and be able to make appropriate and contextual responses based on user input.

- **Natural Grammar** Characters follow correct grammatical rules in language use, pay attention to the dialogue perspective and personal relationships, and need to express clearly and accurately to avoid grammatical errors or structural confusion, making the dialogue smooth and easy to understand.
- **Natural Tone** Character tone should be adjusted based on the context of the conversation and the user's emotional state, ensuring that responses feel like real human interactions and are neither too formal nor too casual.
- **Logical and natural** The character dialogue content should be logical and be able to respond to the user's questions or topics reasonably, avoid jumping thinking or irrelevant answers, and ensure the coherence and rationality of the dialogue.

## When to use

The principle of naturalness is particularly applicable to application scenarios that require frequent and complex communication with human users. The following are some typical application scenarios:

- **Customer Service and Support:** In e-commerce, finance, communications and other industries, AI Agent can automatically answer user inquiries through natural language processing technology, handle order issues and return requests, and provide 7\*24 online services to improve customer satisfaction and efficiency.
- **Smart Assistant and Voice Interaction:** AI Agent integrated into smart speakers and mobile applications needs to be able to interact through voice commands, which requires the Agent to be able to understand and process natural language voice commands.
- **Intelligent tutoring and Q&A:** In the field of education, Agent can provide 24-hour online Q&A service to help students solve problems encountered in learning. Through natural language processing technology, it can understand students' questions and provide accurate answers.
- **Emotional analysis and support:** Through emotional analysis technology, Agent can identify the emotional state of students and provide corresponding support and encouragement to create a positive learning environment.
- **Simulated Society and Metaverse:** In simulated society or metaverse, Agents need to interact with other individuals in the environment in a natural way, including cooperation, competition, and social behaviors.

In these scenarios, the design and implementation of AI characters need to pay special attention to the naturalness and humanization of their communication to improve user acceptance and satisfaction. By simulating human communication methods and behavioral patterns, AI can better integrate into human work and living environments, providing a more natural and intuitive interactive experience.

## Best cases

### 1. Give the role rich, coherent natural language

Set clear characters and goals for the AI. This includes defining the AI's personality traits, behavioral habits, and its role in specific scenarios. These settings will provide the foundation for subsequent designs. Based on the basic background settings, AI characters can be further given more specific skills, emotions, language, and other settings, making the character definition richer and more three-dimensional.

### 2. Provide more realistic conversation references
