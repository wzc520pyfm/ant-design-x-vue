# Introduction

![](https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*ohp_SLO1eaEAAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/fmt.webp)

The user's vague intentions are gradually focused and dismantled by the AI ​​through conversation, and the user's various operation instructions are usually in the form of interactive cards throughout the conversation flow. Consistency between conversational style and role is also key to the AI ​​experience. In addition, behind every good natural conversation experience between humans and machines, there is actually a set of implicit and systematic experience rules hidden. These are exactly what conversation design needs to define.

## WHY conversation design

After clarifying the user’s intention and AI role positioning, you can start designing the conversation process. This process not only involves an in-depth understanding of the user's intention, which helps to design more targeted and effective dialogues based on the user's intention, but also involves an accurate grasp of the AI ​​role. Based on the AI ​​role's personality, the appropriate language style can be selected to build a more realistic and convincing dialogue scene.

![](https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*C94kQK-GA9QAAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/fmt.webp)

In the process of interpersonal communication, both parties in communication often follow a series of implicit or explicit rules in order to achieve specific communication goals. These rules not only cover the semantic level of language, but also include the expression of behavior and intention, thereby effectively advancing the dialogue process and achieving the expected communication purpose.

Similarly, in human-computer interaction, the dialogue system also needs to follow a set of rules to ensure accurate communication of semantics, reasonable display of behaviors, and clear expression of intentions. These rules play a vital role in promoting the realization of user goals and improving the interactive experience. Through carefully designed dialogue rules, the performance of the human-computer dialogue system can be optimized to make it closer to the fluency and efficiency of natural language communication, thereby providing users with a better interactive experience.

## WHAT is conversation design

![What is session design](https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*QI3xQrObsI4AAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/fmt.webp)

Dialogue interaction components are the core content of building a human-computer dialogue system. They generate responses based on preset rules based on questions raised by users. These components are designed to accurately convey semantics, behavior, and intent to facilitate the realization of user goals, and constitute the basic unit of conversational interaction.

**Use different dialogue interaction components to form diverse dialogue expressions: **

- By selecting and combining different dialogue interaction components, a variety of dialogue expression strategies can be constructed. These strategies can not only cover a wide range of language styles and contexts, but also adapt to the individual needs and preferences of users.

![](https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*QROUQbGlvcwAAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/fmt.webp)

**For the same user request, different dialogue design components can be combined to form dialogues with the same semantics and different styles:**

- Semantic consistency and style diversity: Even in the face of the same user request, by flexibly using different dialogue design components, you can create dialogue responses that are consistent in semantics but unique in style. This design allows AI to provide diverse user experiences while maintaining the accuracy of information delivery.

![](https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*2q8gQZMp9l4AAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/fmt.webp)

Therefore, the design and application of dialogue interaction components are the key to achieving efficient and personalized human-computer dialogue. By carefully building and optimizing these components, the performance of the dialogue system and the user's interactive experience can be significantly improved.

## HOW principles

In human-computer dialogue interaction, although dialogue interaction components have the advantages of naturalness and simplified operation paths, they also face some challenges. For example, inaccuracies in intent recognition may lead to incorrect replies, and the lack of slot information may require multiple rounds of dialogue to complete the information. The core goal of conversational interaction is to solve user problems and improve efficiency, and any conversational interaction design should follow this principle. In response to these problems, we have formulated universal principles for dialogue interaction, aiming to optimize dialogue design, give full play to its advantages while avoiding disadvantages, and solve users' actual problems more effectively. Through the following principles, a more efficient, accurate and user-friendly dialogue interaction system can be built.

### 1. Information should be complete and truthful

![](https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*h_9JQLDK_JIAAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/fmt.webp)

In human-computer dialogue interaction, it is necessary to ensure that real information is provided to users, establish trust between users and AI, and emphasize user-centeredness.

#### 1. The AI must provide truthful information

</ImagePreview>

The conversational interaction system must provide information based on facts and data to ensure that users can make decisions based on true and accurate information. This requires AI to indicate the referenced data source when processing user requests and ensure the timeliness and accuracy of the information.

#### 2. The AI must communicate its limits

<ImagePreview>
<img class="preview-img no-padding" src="https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*83p9R7JFirgAAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/fmt.webp">
</ImagePreview>

AI should clearly inform users of its functions and limitations to prevent users from misunderstanding AI capabilities. This includes honestly explaining the situation to the user and providing alternatives or suggestions when the AI ​​is unable to provide a definite answer or perform a specific task.

#### 3. Provide targeted information

<ImagePreview>
<img class="preview-img no-padding" src="https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*BEkxTZKqypgAAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/fmt.webp">
</ImagePreview>

The dialogue subjects should be targeted and provide specialized support for specific decision-making issues and decision-makers. This means that AI needs to be able to provide customized information and recommendations based on the specific needs of the user to enhance the relevance and effectiveness of decision-making

### 2. Wording should be clear and easy to understand

![](https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*sLjARY7FmvcAAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/fmt.webp)

In dialogue design, the wording used should be easy for users to remember, understand, and clearly express meaning, thereby achieving more effective communication.

#### 1. Task relevance

The dialogue content should closely revolve around the user's tasks and goals, ensuring the relevance of information to increase the user's attention and retention.
