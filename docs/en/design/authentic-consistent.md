# Authentic and consistent

## How to define

To increase the credibility and realism of a character, the visual expression and personality traits of the character need to remain consistent across different scenarios. Specifically, the character's behavior patterns and language style should align with the fundamental background of the character. During interactions with the user, maintain the character's consistency across different conversational contexts to avoid a sense of dissonance for the user, thereby continuously delivering an interaction experience that meets expectations.

- **Consistency of personality traits** Emphasize the consistency between the character's inner world and outward behavior. Actions, reactions, decisions, and emotional expressions should match the character's described personality. Whether in calm daily interactions or tense plot developments, the character's personality traits should remain consistent to enhance credibility and depth.
- **Consistent visual expression** The character's appearance design, clothing selection, action performance and expression changes remain coherent and consistent in different scenes and time points. This includes elements such as colour, graphics, gesture and style to ensure a unified character image so that the audience can easily identify and remember the character.
- **Consistent language style** A character’s way of speaking, word choice, tone, and delivery habits should match their personality, background, and emotional state. Whether in a formal setting or an informal exchange, your character's verbal expressions should be consistent to enhance the character's personality and sense of authenticity.
- **Consistent Behavioral Patterns** Behavioral responses and responses to different situations are consistent and predictable. The character's behavior pattern should be coordinated with his or her personality traits, background story, and current situation to ensure that every decision and action of the character in the development of the story appears reasonable and natural.

## When to use

The principle of role consistency applies to educational tutoring, online consultation, entertainment interaction, and any AI scenario that requires in-depth interaction with users. In these scenarios, role consistency can help users better understand the intentions and functions of AI, thereby improving interaction efficiency and satisfaction.

- **Virtual Assistant:** A highly consistent virtual assistant can help users build trust in them. When users know what kind of response and assistance they can expect, they feel more secure and view the virtual assistant as a reliable source of information and problem-solving partner.
- **Customer Service:** Consistency in language, behavior, and knowledge base ensures that customers receive the same level of service in every interaction, which helps improve overall service quality.
- **Game Characters:** A character that is always funny and friendly can enhance the user's entertainment experience. The consistency of the character's background, personality, and image can deepen the identity of the character and make the character more realistic and immersive.
- **Educational Coaching:** A character who remains patient and professional at all times can help users absorb knowledge better. Consistent performance also helps users feel coherence and reliability during the tutoring process, reducing learning anxiety and stress.
- **Health Consultant:** Professional and stable interactive performance can effectively enhance users' trust in health consultants, promote users to actively participate in the treatment process, and ultimately achieve better health management results.
- **Psychological consultation:** Characters who are consistent in their words and deeds and emotionally stable can help build users' trust in the characters, making users feel safe and more willing to open up and share their deepest feelings and troubles.

## Best cases

### 1. Give the role distinctive personality, behavior, and language that match its background

The setting of **character traits, behavior patterns, and language style** are all derived from the character's story background. We can set a detailed and logical character background for the character, so that users can feel the depth and three-dimensionality of the character when they understand the character. Such as some "basic attributes", name, gender, age, nickname, etc. You can also specify a "profession" for the character, such as lawyer, doctor, customer service, etc. You can also give the character a background story, birth environment, growth experience, education experience, family situation, etc.

On the basis of the background story, the character traits, behavior patterns, and language style can be analyzed and set in more detail to ensure that the character has a consistent experience from the inside out.

### 2. Design a unified visual identity that matches the role background

Design a unified image for a character that matches its background characteristics, allowing users to capture and reinforce the character's symbolic memory and form a unified impression. You can design the character's external image in detail from aspects such as image type, physical features, voice tone, and behavioral actions.
