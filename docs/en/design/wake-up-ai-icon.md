# Wake · AI mark

![](https://mdn.alipayobjects.com/huamei_iwk9zp/afts/img/A*qCaWR6G8J4oAAAAAAAAAAAAADgCCAQ/fmt.webp)

AI needs some representative visual symbols to distinguish it from human avatars or other icons.

## Design goals

Using image logos representing AI in product interfaces can attract users’ attention and open up new explorations. Artificial intelligence visual symbols should be highly recognizable and can quickly attract users’ attention among many images, allowing users to identify the artificial intelligence at a glance. It is both simple and versatile, easy to be understood and accepted by different users, considers coordination with different platforms and interfaces, and can be naturally integrated into various digital environments to provide users with a consistent and smooth visual experience.

---

## AI mark options

Consistent image design increases predictability and trust among users and consumers. As AI use cases continue to evolve, we’ll see icon usage evolve with them. The four-pointed star flash point is very common and usually represents the function of AI. It is also used to distinguish information generated by or with the help of AI.
